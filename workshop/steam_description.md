# Boxer +

**Boxer +** (BXP) is a small patch for [GTK Boxer](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3780478952) by Hawkeye that brings BWA3 content onto the Boxer.

## Requirements

- [GTK Boxer](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3780478952)
- [BWMod (BWA3)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1200127537)
- [CBA_A3](https://github.com/CBATeam/CBA_A3)
- [ACE3](https://github.com/acemod/ACE3)

## Features

- **BWA3 Spike LR on the Boxer IFV** — replaces the Titan AT missile on the IFV, including the Sand, Winter, AUS and Medical variants. After launch you get the missile's TV camera and steer it with the mouse; the missile follows a lofted flight profile. Loadout stays 2 + 2, and the launcher flaps open and close as before.
- **BWA3 crews** — Panzertruppe crew in the Woodland / Winter Boxers, tropical crew in the Sand ones, and a BWA3 medic (Sanitäter) crewing the Medical. The AUS Boxers keep the vanilla crew.
- **BWA3 Puma inventory** — the HMG, GMG, IFV and RCH155 carry the Puma's cargo: G36 and MG4 ammo, DM25 / DM32 grenades, G36 and MG4 with pointer, 2 PzF 3 Tandem and first aid kits. The Medical and the AUS Boxers are excluded.
- **Rear view (proof of concept)** — using ACE's viewports, look at the ramp from a passenger seat to see the Boxer's reverse camera. On the Medical only the commander and medic seats have it; the RCH155 does not.
